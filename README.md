**Free forever, no license required!**

Generate professional QR codes instantly within your Figma workflow. With extensive customization options, you can create QR codes that perfectly match your brand identity and design aesthetic.

Works seamlessly across all Figma applications.

## Style Options:

Corner Styles, Center Styles: Square, and Cell Styles

- **Add your brand logo** to the center of the QR code
- **Select colors** of your choice
  Or go with nice plain QR code

## Placements in designs:

- **Add to Selected Element** - If you have a frame, group, or component selected, the QR code will be added inside it
- **Create New on Canvas** - If nothing is selected, a new QR code frame is created on the canvas
