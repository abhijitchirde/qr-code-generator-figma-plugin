Transform any text or link into beautiful, customizable QR codes directly in Figma. Whether you're designing marketing materials, presentations, or interactive prototypes, this plugin makes QR code generation effortless and stylish.

✨ WHY CHOOSE THIS PLUGIN?

Stop switching between external tools and websites. Generate professional QR codes instantly within your Figma workflow. With extensive customization options, you can create QR codes that perfectly match your brand identity and design aesthetic.

🚀 UNIVERSAL COMPATIBILITY

Works seamlessly across all Figma applications:
• Figma Design - Create QR codes for your designs and prototypes
• FigmaJam - Add QR codes to your brainstorming sessions and workshops  
• Figma Slides - Embed QR codes in your presentations
• Figma Buzz - Include QR codes in your interactive content

🎨 POWERFUL CUSTOMIZATION

Color Control:
• Global Color - Set a base color that applies to all QR elements
• Individual Element Colors - Fine-tune colors for corner squares, center pattern, individual cells, and background

Style Options:
• Corner Styles: Square, Rounded, Squircle, Dot, Classy, Classy Rounded
• Center Styles: Square, Dot
• Cell Styles: Square, Rounded, Squircle, Dots, Classy, Classy Rounded

Brand Integration:
• Custom Logo Support - Add your brand logo to the center of the QR code for perfect brand consistency

📤 EXPORT OPTIONS

• PNG Export - Always available, perfect for raster graphics
• SVG Export - Available when no logo is added, ideal for scalable vector graphics

⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE: When a logo is added, only PNG export is available to ensure logo quality. Without a logo, you can export as both PNG and SVG.

🎯 SMART PLACEMENT

• Add to Selected Element - If you have a frame, group, or component selected, the QR code will be added inside it
• Create New on Canvas - If nothing is selected, a new QR code frame is created on the canvas

🌓 USER EXPERIENCE

• Dark & Light Mode - Toggle between themes to match your Figma interface preference
• Live Preview - See your QR code update in real-time as you customize
• Reset Styles - Quickly return to default settings with one click

📝 HOW TO USE

1. Enter Your Content - Type or paste any text or URL into the input field
2. Customize Your QR Code - Choose styles, adjust colors, and optionally add your logo
3. Export - Click "Add as PNG" or "Add as SVG" to place the QR code on your canvas

💡 PRO TIPS

• Use contrasting colors between the QR code and background for better scanability
• Test your QR code after adding a logo to ensure it still scans correctly
• SVG format is great for print and high-resolution displays
• PNG format works best when you need to include a logo
